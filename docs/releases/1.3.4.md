# Pages 1.3.4

This release improves PDF reading and narration, and simplifies reader appearance settings.

- Restores Edge natural voices on Android through the working speech endpoint. Reuses a connection across sentences so PDF narration starts quickly and keeps playing without switching to the phone voice after a transient handshake failure.
- Tracks the spoken word in original PDF view using speech word events and PDF text positions, rather than estimated timing and evenly spaced letters.
- Adds an inferred table of contents for PDFs without embedded chapter bookmarks. For example, *Fallen* now indexes its opening, 20 chapters and epilogue.
- Improves PDF reflow, reading from the selected page, and continuous original-page scrolling. EPUB and PDF scrolling load nearby content more reliably.
- Gives Read mode its own settings page, and removes the old media controls menu from that mode.
- Tidies the muted Preferences layout, narrows top-navigation choices, and fixes playlist and series card appearance to the app defaults.
